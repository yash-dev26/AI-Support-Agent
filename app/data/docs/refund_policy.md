# Refund Policy

Orders can be refunded within 14 days of delivery if the item is unopened and in original packaging.

Digital goods and gift cards are non-refundable once delivered.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

If a customer reports a duplicate or incorrect charge, this requires manual verification by the billing team and should be escalated to a human support agent rather than resolved automatically.

Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the order arrived damaged or incorrect.
